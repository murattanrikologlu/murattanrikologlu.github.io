AI Processing and How We Handle Face Data

To generate the videos and photos you request, the photos, videos and text prompts you provide are sent to third-party AI service providers solely for the purpose of processing your request.

  • Retention of face data: The Application does not perform facial recognition and does not create, collect or retain face data such as facial geometry or biometric identifiers. Your photos are processed as ordinary images.
  • Reason for storing photos: Photos and videos you upload are kept only for as long as needed to generate the video or photo you requested. Generated results are kept in your History so that you can view and download them.
  • Length of time photos are stored: Uploaded input files are deleted from our servers within 24 hours. Generated videos and photos remain in your History until you delete them.
  • Third-party sharing: To generate your content, the photos, videos and text prompts you provide are sent to the AI service providers OpenAI, Google Gemini and Replicate.
  • Reasons for sharing with third parties: These providers process your content solely to produce the video or photo you requested. Your photos are never used for advertising, marketing or profiling.
  • Third parties storing photos: No third party stores your photos for its own purposes. The AI service providers process your content only to generate your request, under their own privacy policies linked below.
