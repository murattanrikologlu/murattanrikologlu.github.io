Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Lorvo app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Murat Tanrikologlu (collectively, the "Application"). Murat Tanrikologlu is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


Summary

Lorvo is a translation application. To produce a translation, the content you choose to translate (the text you type, the photo you capture or select, and your voice while you are using a voice feature) is sent to third-party artificial intelligence services, specifically OpenAI and Google. This is the core function of the Application and it cannot work offline.

The Application asks for your explicit consent inside the app, and explains what is sent and to whom, before any of this content is sent for the first time. If you do not consent, the translation features are not used and no content is sent. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting the Service Provider or by uninstalling the Application.

The Application does not require an account. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or postal address in order to work. Your conversations, photos and voice recordings are not stored by the Service Provider.


Artificial Intelligence Processing and Third-Party AI Services

The table below describes, for each feature, exactly what data leaves your device, who receives it, how it is transmitted, and how long it is kept.

  • Live voice conversation (Voice): While a voice conversation is active, the audio captured by your microphone is streamed directly from your device to Google (Google Gemini Live API) using a short-lived access token issued by the Service Provider's server. This audio does not pass through the Service Provider's server. It is used only to recognise what was said and to return the spoken translation in real time. The Service Provider does not record, store or log this audio. The written transcript shown on screen exists only in the memory of your device during the session and is discarded when the session ends.
  • Voice dictation (Text tab): When you use the microphone to dictate text, the audio captured by your microphone is streamed directly from your device to OpenAI using a short-lived client key (valid for approximately 60 seconds) issued by the Service Provider's server. This audio does not pass through the Service Provider's server. It is used only to convert your speech into text. The Service Provider does not record, store or log this audio.
  • Text translation: The text you enter or dictate is sent to the Service Provider's server and forwarded to OpenAI for the sole purpose of translating it and producing the alternative phrasings and usage examples shown beneath the translation. The text is processed in memory only, for the duration of that single request. It is not saved to any database or file storage, it is not written to logs, and it is not retained after the translation has been returned.
  • Camera translation (Scan): The photo you capture or select is sent to the Service Provider's server and forwarded to OpenAI for the sole purpose of reading and translating the text contained in that image. The image is processed in memory only, for the duration of that single request. It is not saved to any database or file storage, it is not written to logs, and it is not retained after the translation has been returned.
  • Read aloud (text-to-speech): When you press play, the text to be spoken is sent to the Service Provider's server and forwarded to OpenAI for the sole purpose of generating synthetic speech audio, which is streamed back to your device. The text is processed in memory only and is not stored or logged. The voice you hear is artificially generated; it is not the voice of a real person.
  • On-device processing: Detecting where lines of text appear inside a photo is performed on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Ending-of-speech detection in "wait for sentence" mode is performed on your device using a speech-activity model bundled with the Application. Neither of these sends any data off your device.

Use of your content by the AI providers. The Service Provider uses the paid, commercial service tiers of OpenAI and Google. Under the terms of those tiers, content submitted through the interface is not used to train their models. The Service Provider has reviewed the privacy and data-processing terms of both providers and requires them, by contract, to provide a level of protection for your data that is equal to or greater than the protection described in this privacy policy, and to process your data only for the purpose of returning the requested translation.

Links to the privacy policies of the AI providers:

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy and OpenAI Business Terms
  • Google Privacy Policy and Google Cloud Privacy Notice

Please do not submit highly sensitive information. Because the content you translate is sent to a third-party AI service, you should avoid entering passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive personal information into the Application.


Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require you to create an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number or postal address in order to use it.

The following information is collected and used:

  • Device identifier: When you first open the Application, a random identifier is generated on your device and stored in the device keychain. It is not the device serial number, advertising identifier or any identifier provided by Apple, and it cannot be used to identify you personally. It is used to keep track of your subscription status, your remaining free translations for the day, and whether you have already been asked to rate the Application.
  • Anonymous authentication identifier: An anonymous Firebase Authentication identifier is created so that the Service Provider's server can reject requests that do not come from the Application. It is not linked to any account and contains no personal information.
  • Subscription status: Whether you have an active subscription, and which plan, is received from the App Store through RevenueCat and stored against your device identifier so that paid features can be unlocked.
  • Usage counters: The number of translations you have made within the current daily allowance, and the time at which that allowance resets.
  • Technical server logs: Like any internet service, the Service Provider's servers automatically record technical information for each request, which may include the Internet Protocol (IP) address the request came from, the time of the request, the Application version and the operating system version. These logs are used only to operate the service, diagnose faults and prevent abuse. The content you translate is never written to these logs.

The Application does not collect your location, does not access your contacts, does not read your photo library beyond the single picture you choose, and does not collect biometric or health data.


Cookies and tracking technologies

The Application does not use cookies. The Application does not contain advertising, does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and does not use an advertising identifier. No analytics or crash-reporting service is currently active in the Application.

Camera and Photo Processing

When you use the Application's camera translation feature, the photo you capture or select is sent to the Service Provider's server and forwarded to OpenAI, a third-party artificial intelligence service, for the sole purpose of reading and translating the text contained in that image.

The image is processed in memory only, for the duration of that single request. It is not saved to any database or file storage, it is not written to logs, and it is not retained after the translation has been returned. Images are not used for any other purpose, are not shared with any other party, and are not sold.

The Application does not detect, analyze, or store faces. It does not collect biometric identifiers, facial geometry, or any face data that could be used to identify a person, and it does not attempt to identify people appearing in the images.

The Application accesses only the single picture you take with the camera or choose from your device. It does not read, scan, or upload the rest of your photo library.


Microphone and Voice Processing

The Application uses the microphone only while you are actively using a voice feature: during a live voice conversation, or while you are dictating text. The microphone is not used at any other time and the Application does not listen in the background.

As described above, this audio is streamed directly from your device to the relevant AI provider (Google for live voice conversations, OpenAI for dictation) and is not routed through, recorded by, or stored by the Service Provider. No audio file is written to your device.


Third Party Access

The Service Provider shares data with the third parties listed below, only to the extent needed to operate the Application. The Service Provider does not sell your personal data and does not share it for advertising purposes.

  • OpenAI — text translation, camera translation, voice dictation and text-to-speech. Receives the content you choose to translate.
  • Google (Gemini API) — live voice conversation translation. Receives the audio captured during an active voice conversation.
  • Google Firebase — hosting of the Service Provider's server functions, database, anonymous authentication, remote configuration and abuse prevention (App Check). Receives the device identifier, subscription status and usage counters.
  • RevenueCat — management of subscriptions purchased through the App Store. Receives the device identifier and purchase information provided by the App Store.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy, linked above. The Service Provider has reviewed those terms and requires each provider to protect your data to a standard equal to or greater than that described in this privacy policy.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains data based on its necessity for the stated purposes:

  • Content you translate (text, photos, voice audio): not retained. Processed in memory for the duration of the single request and then discarded.
  • Voice conversation transcripts: not retained. They exist only in the memory of your device during the session and are discarded when the session ends.
  • Device identifier, subscription status and usage counters: retained for as long as you use the Application, and for up to 12 months thereafter, unless you request earlier deletion or longer retention is required by law.
  • Technical server logs: retained for up to 30 days for fault diagnosis and abuse prevention.
  • Data required for legal compliance: retained as long as required by applicable law.

You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal obligation to retain it. If you want the Service Provider to delete data associated with your device, please contact them at dev.murattan@icloud.com.

Security

The Service Provider is concerned about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information.
